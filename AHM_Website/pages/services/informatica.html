Focused service sections for the core Informatica and IDMC capabilities in the modernization roadmap.

CDI

Cloud Data Integration

Design and build IDMC CDI pipelines for batch, CDC, API, file, SaaS, database, warehouse, and lakehouse integration patterns.

  • Reusable mapping templates and parameterized job patterns.
  • Source-to-target validation, reconciliation, and restartability.
  • Operational monitoring for failures, latency, and SLA health.
CDG

Cloud Data Governance

Set up governance foundations across catalog, lineage, glossary, stewardship, ownership, policy workflows, and data asset discovery.

  • Business glossary and data ownership model.
  • Technical lineage connected to integration and reporting assets.
  • Stewardship queues for definitions, issues, and approvals.
MDM

Master Data Management

Create trusted master records for customer, product, supplier, location, or finance domains with durable governance around golden data.

  • Domain modeling, match rules, merge rules, and survivorship logic.
  • Hierarchy, relationship, and cross-reference management.
  • Publishing patterns for analytics, operations, and downstream apps.
Quality

Data Quality

Ensures accurate, consistent, and reliable data by detecting and correcting quality issues.

  • Ensures accurate, consistent, and reliable data across systems.
  • Detects and fixes missing, duplicate, and invalid records
  • Standardizes and validates data using defined rules
